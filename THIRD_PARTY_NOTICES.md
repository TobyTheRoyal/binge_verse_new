# Third-Party Data and Assets

- Movie, TV, episode and image metadata: [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org), used through its API. See the [API FAQ and attribution requirements](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq). BINGE does not claim ownership of supplied posters or film imagery.
- Streaming availability: JustWatch data via TMDB; source links appear in the title detail pages and Credits.
- Optional additional ratings: [OMDb](https://www.omdbapi.com).
- `public/tmdb.svg`: unmodified TMDB logo by Travis Bell, obtained from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tmdb.new.logo.svg), documented there as [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The source page identifies the original official TMDB asset. No color, shape or aspect-ratio changes were made.
- Typography: Manrope and Outfit, delivered through Google Fonts; retain their applicable font licenses if self-hosting them.
- UI dependencies and development tools retain their own package licenses.

Demo personal ratings and viewing activity are fictional fixtures. A portfolio deployment is not a grant of rights for a later commercial product: verify the current API, asset and commercial-use terms before that launch.
